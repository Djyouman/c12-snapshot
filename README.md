# C12 Member Snapshot

A mobile-first digital version of the C12 **Member Snapshot** — the Life & Leadership
Balance Wheel, 5-Point Alignment Assessment, and 30-day recovery planning — for use
at the monthly peer group table.

**Live app:** https://djyouman.github.io/c12-snapshot/

## What it does

- **Snapshot** — snap a photo of your paper sheet, score the 8-area Balance Wheel
  (tap the wheel), assess the business (Behind / On / Ahead of target), log big wins.
- **Trends** — month-over-month wheel comparison, per-area sparklines, and an
  early-warning panel for areas *moving the wrong direction* before they get low.
- **30-Day Plans** — any area at 5 or below (or Behind Target, or sliding) requires
  an honest "why is this low" before the plan builder unlocks: 30-day target, a
  first step within 72 hours, four weekly commitments, an accountability partner,
  and a check-in date. AI-drafted plans and a "Coach me in Claude" hand-off included.
  Last month's commitments resurface for a close-the-loop review.
- **Share** — auto-generated talking points for the table, plus one tap to text
  your commitments to your accountability partner.

## Privacy

All data (scores, photos, plans) lives in the browser's local storage on each
member's own phone. Nothing is uploaded anywhere. Use **Export** for backups and
**Add to Home Screen** on iPhone so Safari never evicts your history.

Multiple members can share one device — each profile keeps its own history.
